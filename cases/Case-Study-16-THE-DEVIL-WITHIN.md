# Case Study 16: "The Devil Within" — The Velvet Trap

## 1. Objective
Technical implementation of the **Acoustic Noir** framework within the **RHA Protocol**. This experiment focuses on the pathology of guilt, cyclical self-destruction, and cognitive dissonance—using minimalist sound design and extreme acoustic contrast to simulate a "velvet trap."

## 2. Technical Architecture
* **BPM Dynamics:** Slow-to-mid tempo (82–88 BPM). A controlled slow-burn tension curve designed without explosive or theatrical drops.
* **Frequency Management:** Focus on low-frequency trip-hop textures (muffled kick/brushed snare), warm fingerpicked acoustic guitar, deep sub-bass pads, and cello swells to create a claustrophobic acoustic vacuum.
* **Vocal Interplay & Contrast:** High-contrast dynamic interplay between a cold, objective baritone lead and a fragile, echoing, reverb-heavy chorus timbre to build visceral unease.

## 3. Biological Integrity Audit
* **Quantization:** 0% (Unquantized fingerpicked acoustic guitar and dynamic acoustic elements).
* **Vocal Processing:** Zero pitch correction. Mandatory preservation of natural vocal rasp, friction, fry, and breath dynamics as a rejection of generative/sterile perfection.
* **Acoustic Signatures:** Natural room resonance, organic string friction, tactile acoustic pick plucks, and sparse cold piano accents to ground the track in the physical realm.

## 4. Semantic Metadata
* **Entity Role:** The Shadow Auditor / Pathologist of the Soul.
* **Archetype:** The Objective Mirror / Cognitive Dissonance.
* **Environment:** Psychological Thriller / Velvet Trap / Clinical Isolation.

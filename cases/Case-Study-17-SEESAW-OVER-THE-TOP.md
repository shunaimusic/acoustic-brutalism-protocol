# Case Study 17: "Seesaw Over the Top" — The Broken Mechanism

## 1. Objective
Technical implementation of the **Acoustic Brutalism** framework within the **RHA Protocol**. This experiment functions as a monument to memory—utilizing Acoustic Brutalism to frame memory not as light nostalgia, but as a heavy, rusted mechanism. The focus is on forced adultification, emotional survival, and the physical destruction of childhood mechanics, using foley sound design to simulate a broken system.

## 2. Technical Architecture
* **BPM Dynamics:** Slow tempo (80 BPM). A claustrophobic, slow-burn tension curve that deliberately avoids theatrical drops, building strictly from a sparse acoustic vacuum into a massive, heavily-layered wall of sound.
* **Frequency Management:** Focus on deep analog sub-bass pulses, minimalist muffled trip-hop kicks, distant cold industrial pads, and the haunting, cyclical metallic foley of a music-box celesta to create an industrial, heavy atmosphere.
* **Vocal Interplay & Contrast:** Extreme dynamic dissonance between a raw, stoic male baritone lead and a ghostly, high-pitched children’s choir. This physical clash represents the heavy gravity of grim reality colliding with the ethereal echo of lost innocence.

## 3. Biological Integrity Audit
* **Quantization:** 0% on dynamic acoustic elements. Unquantized rhythmic foley and acoustic fingerpicking maintain organic friction and human imperfection against the structural grid.
* **Vocal Processing:** Zero pitch correction. Mandatory preservation of the male lead's natural rasp, fry, and breaking highs as a rejection of synthetic perfection. The choral layers remain deeply human and vulnerable, avoiding plastic etherealization.
* **Acoustic Signatures:** Rhythmic panning of mechanical foley, raw tactile string plucks, heavy room reverb, and somber organic cello swells that ground the mechanical despair in a tangible physical space.

## 4. Semantic Metadata
* **Entity Role:** The Objective Mirror / Stoic Observer.
* **Archetype:** The Survivor / The Broken Balance.
* **Environment:** Psychological Thriller / Rusted Machinery / The Hallway of Yesterday.
